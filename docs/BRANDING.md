# Brand and identity

EnerLint is the first research-software library of [Open Energy Methods](https://github.com/Open-Energy-Methods). The organization-wide visual identity is the founder-approved navy–teal logo.

This research toolkit has not been endorsed by KTH or certified by an accreditation body.

Brand assets for OEM are maintained by the [.github organization repository](https://github.com/Open-Energy-Methods/.github).
